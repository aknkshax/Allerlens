How to read the tags

ContainsListed as an ingredient.
May containPrecautionary statement: possible cross-contact, not a confirmed ingredient.
EvidenceReported in a database or paper (UniProt, IEDB).
PredictionComputational output: a lead to explore, not proof.

Planned sources

UniProt, IEDB, FSSAI, Codex Alimentarius and FAO/WHO.

Known limits

Keyword matching can miss unusual ingredient names and flag harmless words. The demo covers 9 allergen groups and 4 protein pages. "No listed allergen found" is never a safety guarantee. Cross-reactivity notes are general science, not a prediction for any individual.
