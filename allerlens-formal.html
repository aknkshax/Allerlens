About AllerLens

AllerLens was developed by second-year Biotechnology students Keisha Taneja, Madhulekha D, Reva C, and Varsha Sriram as part of their Design Thinking and Methodology (DTM) project. Driven by their interest in immunoinformatics, the team explored real-world problems in the field and identified food allergies as an area of concern.

Through a consumer survey, they discovered that many people struggled to understand allergens, the proteins responsible for allergic reactions, and how to identify potential allergens hidden in packaged food ingredient lists. This inspired the creation of AllerLens, a web-based tool that brings these two aspects together by simplifying food label interpretation while connecting allergens to their underlying protein science.

By bridging the gap between scientific knowledge and everyday food choices, AllerLens aims to make allergen information more accessible, understandable, and transparent, helping users make more informed decisions.
